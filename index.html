WHAT WE DO

Three jobs that usually slip when a business is busy.

We do not replace your people or your tools. We connect what you already use, and handle the repeated steps in between.

Reply fast

A new inquiry gets a clear, polite answer within minutes, with the questions you need answered before a visit or quote.

Keep it organized

Requests from forms, texts and email land in one place with the details your team needs, so nobody copies them by hand.

Follow up

Reminders for visits, check-ins after quotes and a review request once the job is done. Each message is sent only when the rule you agreed on says so.

In plain terms: automation means a set of rules that runs in the background. For example, "if a customer asks for a quote and does not reply in two days, send one follow-up." You decide the rules. We build them, test them and show you what they did.

WORKFLOWS

Six workflows we build most often.

Each one is a chain of steps with a clear start and a clear stop. We build them in tools such as n8n, then test them with your real examples before anything goes live.

Missed-call text-back

TRIGGER: CALL

A call goes unanswered while the crew is on a job. The customer gets a text within a minute and the request is logged.

Missed call→Text sent→Reply captured→Job card

Quote follow-up

TRIGGER: QUOTE SENT

After two days with no reply, one polite follow-up is sent. The sequence stops the moment the customer answers.

Quote sent→Wait 2 days→No reply?→One follow-up

Booking and reminders

TRIGGER: BOOKING

A confirmed visit gets reminders the day before and two hours before. A missed visit is flagged for the office.

Booked→Reminder 24h→Reminder 2h→No-show flag

Review request

TRIGGER: JOB DONE

Two hours after a job is marked complete, the customer gets a review link. A low rating goes to the owner first.

Job complete→Wait 2h→Review link→Rating check

Drawing intake

TRIGGER: EMAIL

Drawings and photos sent by email are saved to a job folder. Missing measurements trigger a short list of questions.

Email + file→Folder by job→Missing details asked

Weekly owner report

TRIGGER: MONDAY 7 AM

Every Monday the owner gets one message: open quotes, bookings for the week, and any request still waiting for an answer.

Pull open items→Summarize→Send to owner

How we tailor a workflow to your business

You bring a blueprint: how your work actually moves today, from the first call to the final invoice. We do not start with a template. We go around the loop until the workflow matches how your team works.

ROUND 1

Blueprint

We map your current process with you on paper or a whiteboard.

ROUND 2

First draft

We build the workflow in a test environment with your sample data.

ROUND 3

Walkthrough

You and your team go through it step by step and mark what is wrong.

ROUND 4

Adjust

We change the rules, wording and timing based on your notes.

ROUND 5

Live test

It runs on real requests for a short period, with your approval on each message.

Each round can go back to the one before it. Nothing goes live until you say so.

QUESTIONS

What owners ask first.

Do I need to change the software I already use?

Usually not. We connect what you have, such as your calendar, email, forms or field service app. The first meeting shows where the gaps are.

Will customers know they are talking to a system?

Automated replies are clearly written as automated and always include a way to reach a real person. You approve the wording before anything goes out.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We explain every step in plain language and train your team on the parts they use. You will not need to write code or manage servers.

What if we tried something like this before and it did not work?

That is useful to know. We start by asking what was tried and why it failed, then design around that. If we cannot improve on what you have, we will say so.

Where are you based?

We are based in Toronto and meet clients across the GTA, in person or by video.
